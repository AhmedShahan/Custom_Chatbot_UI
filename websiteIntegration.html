Website with Local Chatbot Integration

About This Demo

This website demonstrates how to integrate a locally running chatbot into a webpage using a chat bubble interface.

Click the chat bubble in the bottom right corner and enter the ID of your local RAG-based chatbot to connect.

How It Works

The chat bubble opens an overlay that requests your chatbot ID. Once provided, it connects to your local chatbot running at http://0.0.0.1:8501/.

This is ideal for testing chatbot integrations during development before deploying to a production environment.
